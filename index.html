About me

I'm a PhD researcher at the University of Limerick, Ireland, supervised by Prof. Harry Van Den Akker. My work focuses on embedding local DNS periodic boxes in Large Eddy Simulations, with specialisations in Computational Fluid Dynamics, Lattice Boltzmann methods, Turbulence Modelling, and Multiphase flows. I hold an MSc. (Cum Laude) in Chemical Engineering from TU Delft and a B.E. in Chemical Engineering from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad. Outside research, I specialise in landscape, wildlife, and astrophotography.

Projects

DNS in Large Eddy Simulations

PhD project at University of Limerick. Developing a framework for embedding periodic box DNSs in LES, including DNS-informed LES of emulsions using databases. P.I: Prof. Harry Van Den Akker.

Non-isothermal Stochastic Rotation Dynamics

Master's thesis at TU Delft. Built a framework for non-isothermal SRD simulations, novel thermal no-slip boundary conditions, and models for adsorption/reaction/desorption with energy transport. P.I: Prof. Dr. Ir Johan Padding.

Reactive Flow in Tubular Reactors

Honours project at TU Delft & University of Limerick. Modelled reactive flow with varying molecular weight using a variable speed-of-sound Lattice Boltzmann solver. P.I: Prof.Dr. Ir Harry van den Akker.

Drying of Porous Media via LBM

Bachelor's thesis at BITS Pilani. Created a multi-component multi-phase LBM solver for drying in 2D porous media and studied Haines jumps at fluid interfaces. P.I: Prof. Vikranth Surasani.
